Fantasy Playtime is a free companion app that organizes fantasy football around the actual NFL games. Pick a game to see your fantasy players in it, including your starters, bench players, and your opponents’ starters.

Connect teams from ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, and Fleaflicker to follow leagues across platforms in one place, keep up with live scores, and spot unrostered players who are breaking out.
